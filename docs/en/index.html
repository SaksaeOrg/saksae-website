FAQ

Your questions, our answers

Who sees what in SAKSAE?

Everyone has a role, with rights set module by module and action by action. A salesperson sees their deals, an HR manager sees staff files, and you keep the big picture.

How many users can I invite?

It depends on your plan: 1 on Freelancer, up to 9 on Team, up to 20 on Growth, beyond that on quote. The price is fixed for the tier: one more colleague within the tier costs nothing. You add or remove access at any time.

Is my data protected?

Each user has their own access and only sees what their role allows. Salaries and HR data are hidden from everyone else. A meeting is only recorded with every participant’s consent.

Does the AI act without me?

No. SAKSAE suggests, you approve. No email, reminder or task goes out without your click. And the AI follows the same rights: it only relies on what your role lets you see.

What does my accountant see?

Sales, purchases and, if you turn it on, monthly payroll. Never your clients, your meetings or your HR data. Their access is free.

Is there a commitment?

Monthly billing has no commitment. Annual billing costs 20% less.

How do I get started?

Create your account, import your clients from an Excel or CSV file and connect your email. A guided setup walks you through it.
